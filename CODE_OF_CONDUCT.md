# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

**Positive behavior:**
- Being respectful and constructive
- Accepting feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and will result in a response deemed appropriate to the circumstances.
